# Growth Audit — Wildfire Tracker

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/wildfire-tracker-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Wildfire Tracker already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in the app's own design. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the year when they close — yearly on its own, at 5,77 zł a week, next to what Premium adds | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Say what Premium adds — the four extras your App Store listing names | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Ask what they watch, and who for — then show their watch list | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Put their worry in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 05 | Read their answers back while it sets up | CR +10–15% |

Every price, source and feature line comes from Wildfire Tracker's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Wildfire Tracker.
